FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an Agentic AI consultant do?

I build AI agents that do real sales work for small businesses — answering missed calls, following up with leads, booking appointments, and replying to reviews. Not chatbots that deflect. Agents that complete the job, then log it in your CRM.

How much does an AI automation project cost?

It depends on scope. A single system like the missed-call textback is a one-time setup plus a flat monthly; a full AI sales department is a bigger build with a monthly that covers running it. Book a free consult and I'll give you an exact number — no hourly billing, no surprises.

How long does it take to build an AI follow-up system?

The Safety Net goes live in 3 days. A full follow-up and booking system takes about 2 weeks. The voice receptionist takes about 3 weeks. You test everything live before customers ever see it.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. I'm based in Toronto and work remotely with US and Canadian small businesses. Everything is delivered remotely and billed in USD for US clients — no visa, no relocation, no travel needed.

What tools do you build with?

n8n for automation, Claude for the AI brain, Twilio and ElevenLabs for voice, and GoHighLevel for the CRM and follow-up. You own the accounts, the number, and the data — nothing is locked to me.
